Austin's Climate & Your Pool Surface

240+ Sunny Days/Year

Austin averages 240+ sunny days annually with temperatures regularly exceeding 100°F in summer. Intense UV exposure degrades white plaster faster — consider quartz or pebble for longer life in Central Texas.

Moderate Water Hardness

Austin's Colorado River water measures ~200–250 mg/L calcium carbonate (moderately hard). This is gentler than San Antonio's Edwards Aquifer water but still requires regular calcium management to prevent scaling.

Pool Season: 7+ Months

Austin's long pool season (April–October) means your surface works harder. More swim time, more chemical exposure, more UV — all factors that determine how long your surface lasts. Premium surfaces pay off here.

TDLR License Requirements in Austin

Texas has no state license for pool resurfacing contractors. Electrical work on pool equipment must be done under a TDLR electrical license, and most cities, including San Antonio, require permits for new pools. Check any electrical license at tdlr.texas.gov.

How to Verify a TDLR License

Visit tdlr.texas.gov/LicenseSearch/ and search by contractor name or license number. Ask which TDLR license covers any electrical work.

Red Flags to Watch For

Be wary of contractors who: demand full payment upfront, can't name the license that covers the electrical work, have no local reviews, pressure you to sign same-day, or offer prices drastically below other quotes.

Frequently Asked Questions — Austin Pool Resurfacing

How much does pool resurfacing cost in Austin?

Austin pool resurfacing typically costs $5,000–$10,000 for an average 450–500 sq ft pool. White plaster is the most affordable at $3,800–$7,000; pebble aggregate runs $6,500–$12,000. Austin's higher labor costs add 10–15% vs other Texas cities.

What's the best pool surface for Austin's heat?

Given Austin's 240+ sunny days and 100°F+ summers, quartz or pebble aggregate surfaces hold up better than white plaster under intense UV exposure. They're more expensive upfront but last 5–8 years longer in Central Texas conditions.

How long does pool resurfacing take in Austin?

Typically 5–8 days: 1–2 days to drain and prep the surface, 1–2 days for application, and 3–5 days for curing. Avoid scheduling during August when temperatures can slow curing or affect the bonding process.

Do I need a permit for pool resurfacing in Austin?

Generally, resurfacing alone doesn't require a permit in Austin if no structural changes are made. However, if you're adding features (waterfalls, lighting, equipment upgrades), permits may be required. Your TDLR-licensed contractor can advise.
